How CRM runs

Four habits that keep every lead moving.

Capturing, following up, winning and keeping customers. Each one writes to the same customer record that Sales and Finance use.

01 · Leads

Capture

Leads from the website, email, calls and events land in one list with their source. Duplicates are caught, and each one has an owner before anyone has to ask.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Captured with its source

    Website, email, call, event or referral.

  2. 02Checked for duplicates

    Matched to an existing company first.

  3. 03Owner assigned

    By region, industry or a fair rotation.

  4. 04First reply on time

    A timer starts the moment the lead lands.

02 · Follow-ups

Follow up

Every lead has a next step and a date. When one goes quiet, the owner hears first and then the team lead, so nothing waits without someone knowing.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Next step set

    A call, an email or a meeting, with a date.

  2. 02Owner reminded

    On the day, not after the lead has gone cold.

  3. 03Escalated after three days

    The team lead sees what is waiting.

  4. 04Logged on the timeline

    Calls and emails saved where everyone can see them.

03 · Pipeline

Win

Deals move through stages you define, with a value and a chance of winning at each one, so the forecast comes from the pipeline rather than from a guess.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Qualified

    Need, budget and timing recorded.

  2. 02Quote from the record

    Prices and limits from Sales, in a few clicks.

  3. 03Weighted forecast

    Value multiplied by the chance at each stage.

  4. 04Won becomes an order

    Handed to Sales without retyping.

04 · Accounts

Keep

After the first order, the account is watched. Customers who go quiet, renewals coming up and open complaints each raise a task for the owner.

See it in a demo

  1. 01One account timeline

    Orders, invoices, calls and tickets together.

  2. 02Quiet customers flagged

    No contact in 60 days opens a check-in.

  3. 03Renewals ahead

    Contracts due in 90 days reach their owner.

  4. 04Retention reported

    Who stayed, who left and why.

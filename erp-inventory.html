How stock moves

Four movements, kept on one stock record.

Goods in, between locations, out to customers and counted back. Each one updates the same quantities that sales, purchasing and finance read.

01 · Goods in

Receive

Goods are checked against the purchase order as they come off the truck. Scan, count and put away, and the stock is on the system before the driver leaves.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Checked against the order

    Ordered, delivered and short, line by line.

  2. 02Scanned in

    Barcode or RFID, no clipboard.

  3. 03Put away to a bin

    The bin is recorded, so it can be found again.

  4. 04Cost posted

    Stock value and the supplier balance move together.

02 · Locations

Store and transfer

Every warehouse, store and van holds its own stock, and each transfer between them is tracked until it is counted in at the other end.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Stock by location

    What is on hand, at every site, right now.

  2. 02Transfer requested

    From the site that has it to the one that needs it.

  3. 03In transit, on record

    Shown on the road, not lost between two places.

  4. 04Received and counted

    Short or damaged items noted on arrival.

03 · Goods out

Pick and ship

Sales orders become pick lists by bin and route. What leaves the dock reduces stock and drafts the invoice in one step.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Pick list by bin

    Ordered for the shortest walk.

  2. 02Scanned to the order

    A wrong item or quantity stops at the scan.

  3. 03Dispatched

    The delivery note goes with the goods.

  4. 04Invoice drafted

    Stock goes down and the invoice is ready.

04 · Control

Count and replenish

Cycle counts keep the numbers honest, and reorder points watch every item, so you learn an item is running low from the system, not from a customer.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Cycle count by aisle

    The warehouse keeps working while you count.

  2. 02Differences approved

    Nothing changes until someone signs it off.

  3. 03Reorder point reached

    Watched for every item, at every location.

  4. 04Draft order raised

    Sent to purchasing with the quantity worked out.

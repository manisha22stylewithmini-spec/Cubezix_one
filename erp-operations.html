How operations runs

Four steps between the order and the invoice.

Planning, dispatching, doing the work and closing it. Each one reads the order from Sales and drafts the invoice in Finance.

01 · Planning

Plan

Approved orders and booked jobs become tomorrow's plan overnight, with each stop and task given a person, a vehicle and a time window.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Orders in, plan out

    Tomorrow is planned before the office opens.

  2. 02Routes by time and place

    Stops ordered for the fewest kilometres.

  3. 03Crews and vehicles assigned

    Skills, availability and capacity checked.

  4. 04Customers told their window

    By SMS or email, automatically.

02 · Deliveries

Dispatch and track

Drivers and crews see their day on their phone. Status updates as they go, and the one that runs late is caught before the customer calls.

See it in a demo

  1. 01The day on their phone

    Stops, contacts and notes for each one.

  2. 02Live status

    On the way, arrived, done.

  3. 03Late caught early

    Flagged to the owner and the customer.

  4. 04Proof of delivery

    Signature and photo saved on the record.

03 · Jobs

Jobs and service

Installations, maintenance and service requests run as jobs with steps, owners and a response time, linked to the order or contract they belong to.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Ticket or job raised

    By the customer, the office or the system.

  2. 02Assigned with a time to respond

    Urgent work reaches the nearest crew.

  3. 03Steps checked off

    On the phone, on site.

  4. 04Materials logged

    Stock drawn from Inventory against the job.

04 · Job costing

Close and bill

When the work is done, hours, materials and travel are already on the job. The invoice is drafted, and you can see whether the job made money.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Signed off on site

    The customer signs on the crew's phone.

  2. 02Hours and materials totalled

    From the job, not from memory.

  3. 03Invoice drafted

    Sent to finance the moment the job closes.

  4. 04Margin per job

    Planned against actual, for every job.

01 · Why we exist

Most companies we meet run on five tools that never talk. Invoices in one, payroll in another, stock on a spreadsheet and approvals over WhatsApp. Every month someone stays late to make the numbers agree. We think that work should not exist. So we build one place where every record is typed once, every UAE rule is handled by default, and every customer has a name to call.

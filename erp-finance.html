How finance runs

Four flows, kept in one set of books.

Getting paid, paying suppliers, closing the month and reporting. Each one posts to the same ledger as sales, purchasing and payroll.

01 · Receivables

Get paid

Turn every sale into cash you can see. Invoices go out with VAT and your TRN on them, reminders chase what is late, and each payment finds its invoice the moment it lands.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Invoice from the order

    The quote becomes the order, the order becomes the invoice. Nothing is typed twice.

  2. 02VAT on every line

    Your TRN, the tax amount and the e-invoice fields are added as the invoice is raised.

  3. 03Reminders that send themselves

    Overdue invoices are chased on a schedule you set, politely and on time.

  4. 04Payment matched

    The bank line is matched to its invoice and the receivable clears in the ledger.

02 · Payables

Pay suppliers

Pay the right supplier the right amount, once. Every bill is checked against what was ordered and received before anyone approves it.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Bill checked against the order

    Quantities and prices are compared with the purchase order and the goods received.

  2. 02Approved within limits

    The budget owner approves. Larger amounts go to a second approver.

  3. 03Payment run scheduled

    Due bills are grouped into one run, ready for the bank on the right day.

  4. 04Settled and posted

    The payment clears the payable and the bank line matches itself.

03 · Ledger and close

Close the books

One ledger that every module posts to, and a month-end that follows the same checklist every time. When the last line is done, the period locks.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Every module posts here

    Sales, purchasing, payroll and inventory write their own journals.

  2. 02Banks reconciled

    Statement and ledger agree, with the exceptions listed for you.

  3. 03Accruals and adjustments

    Recurring journals post themselves. One-offs go through maker-checker.

  4. 04Period locked

    Once closed, nobody can post into the month without an approved reopen.

04 · Reporting and tax

Report and file

Statements that are always current, budgets against actuals, and a VAT return prepared from the same numbers your auditor will see.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Statements, always current

    Profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flow, with last month beside this one.

  2. 02Budget against actual

    Every cost centre against its budget, with the variance explained.

  3. 03VAT return prepared

    Output and input tax totalled for the period, ready for your review.

  4. 04Audit pack in one export

    Ledgers, reconciliations and the change log, together for your auditor.

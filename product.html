06 · The UAE, built in

The UAE's rules,
built in.

Tax, payroll and currency rules are part of the platform, not a project on top of it. Each line shows the rule and what ONEERP does with it.

Example invoice. Demo rates; on a UAE tax invoice the VAT is also stated in AED.

Invoices can be raised in the currency of the payment. Five of the six Gulf currencies are pegged to the US dollar, which keeps conversion predictable; tax is where the six differ, and Saudi Arabia has required e-invoices through ZATCA's FATOORA system since December 2021.

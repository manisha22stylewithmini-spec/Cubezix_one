How a sale runs

Four stages of one deal.

Quote, order, delivery and invoice. Kept as one record, the price agreed is the price invoiced, and nothing delivered goes unbilled.

01 · Quotation

Quote

A quote in seconds from your price list, with the customer's terms and the discount limits already applied. Above the limit, the right person is asked before it goes.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Priced from the list

    The customer's own prices and terms, if they have them.

  2. 02Discount checked

    Above 15%, it waits for approval.

  3. 03Sent and followed

    Opened, viewed and followed up on time.

  4. 04Every version kept

    Revisions sit on the same record as the original.

02 · Sales order

Order

An accepted quote becomes the order without retyping. Stock is checked before a date is promised, and every change is tracked against what was agreed.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Created from the quote

    Lines, prices and terms carried across.

  2. 02Stock reserved

    The promised quantity is set aside.

  3. 03Credit checked

    Over-limit customers go to finance first.

  4. 04Delivery scheduled

    Operations sees it the moment it is confirmed.

03 · Delivery

Deliver

Picking, delivery notes and proof of delivery sit on the same record as the order, so partial deliveries and returns never get lost between teams.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Picked and packed

    From the warehouse that holds the stock.

  2. 02Delivery note signed

    Proof of delivery kept on the order.

  3. 03Partials tracked

    What is still owed stays visible.

  4. 04Returns handled

    A credit note raised from the original invoice.

04 · Invoice

Invoice and collect

What was delivered is what gets invoiced, at the price that was agreed. The invoice carries VAT and your TRN, and the payment closes the deal.

See it in a demo

  1. 01Invoiced from the delivery

    Quantities from the delivery note, prices from the quote.

  2. 02VAT applied

    5% and your TRN on every invoice.

  3. 03Sent and reminded

    Overdue invoices are chased on schedule.

  4. 04Paid and closed

    The payment matches the invoice in finance.
